Format & Bets

Ryder Cup: Team US 🇺🇸 vs Team EU 🇪🇺

  • Thu: The Cradle (9-hole par 3), skins, kicks things off
  • Fri AM: No. 4 Four-Ball (Best Ball): 4 matches, 2v2, best score on each team counts
  • Fri PM: No. 3 Modified Alternate Shot: 4 matches. Both players tee off, then alternate shots. Handicap is 40% of the difference.
  • Sat: No. 10 Singles: 8 one-on-one matches (with CTPs). Pairings are drawn Friday night.
  • Most points after singles wins the Cup

Sunday: No. 1 Chicago Points

An individual points game. Your quota is based on your handicap, and you earn points for each score: bogey 1, par 2, birdie 4, eagle 8 (standard Chicago). Highest total over your quota wins. Includes CTPs and skins.

Final quotas and rules are set by Joe, Boerger and Bokeno. Ask them if you're unsure.
